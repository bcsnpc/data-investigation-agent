## Platform-literal debt

`adapters` and generated bytecode. Publisher/evaluator fixtures, test examples,
documentation and lower-level transport scripts are outside that engine boundary.

names/values and product names. Shared neutral contract words such as status,
units, COUNT and OPEN are not classified as fixture vocabulary merely because
the fixture also uses them. The scan is lexical, not proof of semantic neutrality.
