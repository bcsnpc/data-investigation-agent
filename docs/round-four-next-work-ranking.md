Why this order: the remaining installation bottleneck is absence of declared paths
in real estates. The committed [binding design](inferred-code-binding-design.md)
addresses that most directly. The manifest is second because those paths need an
explicit identity/role/provenance envelope; deliver that envelope narrowly before
shipping an inference producer, not as a separate large orchestration layer.
Neutrality comes before expanding one adapter's filtered behavior, so that the
new translation contract remains reusable. Ticket history helps operation but
cannot make an unreadable or unfaithful boundary answerable.
