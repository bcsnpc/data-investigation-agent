# Round Three A4: freshness collection does not stop at a transformation

2026-10-04. For FRESHNESS tickets the procedure collects load-history and
source-delivery evidence on each declared job boundary in the resolved path,
even after a different boundary has yielded a transformation explanation.
The adapter identifies declared jobs from discovered definitions; the engine
does not interpret platform kinds or choose a different path. Attempts and
unavailability reasons remain explicit, with original receipts when obtained.
Configured-unreachable application layers are never read.

The collection does not change the independently supported outcome or promote
successful completion to currency. Within-run job accounting is retained by
declared job identity, so the ordinary walk and freshness completion reuse the
same receipt rather than dispatching duplicate reads. Missing evidence still
refuses latency/gap claims. Existing diagnostic/physical caps govern all reads.

Three new regressions cover a transformation divergence before two declared jobs
an unreachable source, and original job-receipt reuse; they prove the additional evidence attempt without
forcing additional quantity descent or reading the unreachable application.
No live run in this PR; no ledger row. Model payload/directory shaping is
unchanged. Prior engine freezes invalidated. A5, A6 and four scenarios remain.
