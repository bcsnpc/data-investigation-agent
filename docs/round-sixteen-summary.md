2026-10-12. This is a bounded implementation round, not a successful general-capability acceptance. Whole-estate scoring is governed by the Part D readiness records below. Draft #423 remains open; no merge and no replacement of historical evidence.

## What changed

- Stable-identity continuation replaces refusal on a changed catalog hash; changed referents require renewed confirmation. Replies receive a fresh bounded hybrid phase. Choices use visible names.
- Additive claim intake retains plural metrics/figures, direction, comparator, record examples, scope and provenance. Settled facts cannot be asked again. Interpretation confirmation precedes execution.
- On-device screenshot OCR records positions and matches retained labels; confirmed portal mappings and glossary links retain provenance. Ambiguous lookalikes remain held. Screenshot filters and numerical tie-breaking are not compiled.
- Confirmed claims can execute bounded set, multiplicity, unique-record value and integral-dimension decomposition checks on faithfully mapped direct Copy paths, plus page consistency and optional refresh/load observations. Original read receipts and consumer recompilation govern every finding.
- Actions, attestation limits and refusal reasons are engine-rendered. Submitted fact construction is independent of JSON object key order. The replay consumer conserves direct claim output pairs.
