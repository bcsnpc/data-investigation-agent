# Question-led reproduction output composition

Date: 2026-10-03 America/Chicago. Follows merged #323; implementation only here.

The engine had answered R2/R3's declared-context reproduction questions, but the
write-up labelled them unanswered because the separate vertical walk stopped at
NO_COMPARABLE_PATH. Business prose also prepended three candidate paragraphs,
hid RECEIPT, and recommended access even though access did not block reproduction.

## Composition rules

One deterministic composition selects the answering cell: a reproduced candidate
first, otherwise the fullest tested restriction set, with stable identity breaking
ties. For selection/context reproduction requests, that result supplies the answer
line and action independently of the vertical outcome. No reported figure means
no verdict and a request for the figure; it does not erase completed quantities.
Other question subjects, including freshness, are not closed by a reproduction.

Business prose leads with the answering cell, actual quantity, displayed restriction
labels and literal values. Other candidates have one line each. No separate
vertical-path quantity is spliced into the business narrative. Its technical
counterpart labels that probe under "What else was checked". The original
classification, support contract, evidence and refusals do not change.

Reproduction recommends an enhancement to unintended report restrictions, not
data changes or access. Non-reproduction names moved slicer first, bookmark,
selection, row-level security and deeper divergence, and asks for information
that can narrow that set. Missing figure asks for the figure or empty state.
Saved-default assumptions and all other reproduction hedges render once.

Identifier validation tests form, not technical-word equality. RECEIPT, Sales and
Customers remain content; URI, GUID, qualified name, path, underscore identifier
and receipt-ID forms remain refused. Native titles are extracted only by the
adapter from retained literal report-definition titles. Missing, ambiguous,
dynamic, oversize or identifier-form titles are not guessed. The engine receives
neutral display names. Tested semantic connection coverage gets a display-only
ceiling clause; this is not new attestation or an elevation attempt.

## Context cost and tests

Preserved R2 provider-view characters: 13,383 before, 15,105 after. R3: 13,490
to 14,883. R1: 7,231 to 7,950. All retain three candidates, respectively 21,
21 and 23 evidence references, and no elision under the unchanged 48,000 cap.
Directory entries and SQL-object entries are zero before and after on this
synthesis-only view; the planner directory is untouched. A ten-candidate,
ten-restriction coverage test asserts no candidate/evidence loss, no elision
and fewer than 24,000 characters after adding display labels and ceiling facts.

Eight composition tests cover reproduction over a refused vertical path,
non-reproduction, missing figure, category-specific actions, one lead/two short
secondary mentions, hedge uniqueness, literal values versus identifiers,
question-first ordering, freshness separation and ceiling wording. Existing
synthesis, scoped-cell, receipt and narrative tests remain applicable. Final
suite and six-check CI results are recorded on the PR before merging.

## Deliberate departures from the illustrative R2 target

The actual renderer calls the comparison value "without applying report
declarations", not "with no restrictions at all": row-level security and other
native context remain unknown. It names the page-and-slicer visual and its 92 in
one secondary line, rather than claiming that its row set is otherwise identical.
It reports a blank calculation, not that every row was excluded: a blank SUM
alone is not a row-count receipt. It therefore describes reproduction by declared
report design without inventing that row-level mechanism or business intent.
The enhancement action names the full tested restriction set; no evidence selects
which restriction the business wants changed.

## Delivery discipline

No investigation, model call, estate read, fixture/configuration/grant/policy or
cap change, or ledger row in this implementation milestone. Prior freezes are
invalidated by changed engine bytes. After merge, separately recorded offline
re-renderings will use the existing validated historical bindings and retained
mechanism paragraphs; original runs and #323 outputs stay unchanged. The fixture
change remains deferred.
