# Part D: mapped value confidence and its limits

Part D adds `VALUE_VERIFIED` as a separate evidence grade. It means an observed scoped aggregate agrees under the recorded mapping, or an attested numeric recomputation explains its difference. It does not establish business correctness, semantic equivalence, individual column values or row-by-row equality. Full `VERIFIED`, its existing resolver and its requirements are unchanged.

The executable attachment bridge currently supports compiled `SUM` plans. It independently recompiles the plan, checks exact successful provider/query receipts and their typed values and surface attestations, and binds them to an existing graph edge. The selected edge must connect the actual semantic quantity column and its explicitly mapped physical column in the correct container. Current graph, model/context, definition, scope, mapping and endpoint pins must match. Missing or changed pins, a forged receipt, a missing edge or an edge with different endpoints refuse attachment; aggregate agreement cannot manufacture lineage.

The proof bundle is retained before confidence publication. The repository then rechecks current pins and appends the distinct grade and its qualifications. Existing evidence and history remain intact. Partial surface attestation remains visibly partial. BLANK is an explicit comparable state, distinct from zero, a failed query and an absent value. Tolerance must be supplied explicitly with its origin; none is inferred.

Support is deliberately narrower than the observational compiler. Billing MRR's `SUMX`/`RELATED` expression can be compiled and observed, but its graph attachment remains `SUMX_RELATED_GRAPH_ATTACHMENT_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`. Indirect chains through query-step nodes also refuse when no exact supported graph edge joins the compared quantities. These attachment gaps are disclosed independently of an earlier native query failure. A grade on selected boundaries is not a complete-path grade; missing required boundaries remain partial.

Every comparison carries the snapshot caveat. Without aligned query-bound served-version evidence on every participating read, `SNAPSHOT_UNVERIFIED` remains; matching standalone metadata or aggregate values does not remove it. The mapping itself is an explicit assumption, not a proof of semantic equivalence.

The prior retained cloud verification encountered native Power BI HTTP 401 (`PowerBINotAuthorizedException`). A failed native read cannot become a successful proof-shaped receipt, and SQL-side success cannot establish native authorization or earn the paired value grade. No new Part D live result is asserted here: the live checks are pending at this document's preparation, and their exact outcomes will be appended to the round record. Offline synthetic tests demonstrate the reachable trusted SUM success path and hostile-input refusals; they do not demonstrate a grade earned on the live estate.

The value-response tape replays compilation and recorded query responses. Graph publication has a separately retained proof bundle and is not claimed to be covered by that response replay.
